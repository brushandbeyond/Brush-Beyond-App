## Review notes

No account credentials, subscriptions, or purchases are required. Create a device-local profile with any nickname and avatar to open lessons.

YouTube is disabled by default. Open Parents & carers, complete the displayed arithmetic adult check, then review and explicitly allow YouTube access. No fixed passcode is required; each question is generated locally. External destinations require a further adult check, including link-outs from the iOS video player.

To test badges, watch a main lesson video to its end with at least 95% watched coverage. Seeking to the end, pausing without viewing, or opening YouTube externally does not qualify. For tutorials, also advance to the last written step. Previously earned badges are retained.

Profile deletion is on My Profile and requires confirmation. Progress and parent choices are stored on the device. Written lesson content and cover images are bundled. Video playback requires internet access.

Review contact name and telephone must be entered by the account owner. Do not use the support email as a substitute for Apple's required review contact fields.
